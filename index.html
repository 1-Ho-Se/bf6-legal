Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

BF6 Clips is a personal tool used by a single person to publish their own Battlefield 6 gameplay videos to their own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public.

  • Data used: the access tokens of the owner's own accounts, the owner's username, and basic information about the videos the owner publishes.
  • Purpose: only to upload and publish videos to the owner's accounts and to check the status and statistics of those posts.
  • Storage: all data stays on the owner's personal computer. It is never shared with third parties, never sold and never used for advertising.
  • Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time from each platform's settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services) and delete the local data.
  • Contact: send a direct message to the BF6 Clips account on TikTok.

YouTube API Services

  • BF6 Clips uses the YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to read the statistics of those videos (views, likes, comments, watch time).
  • By using BF6 Clips you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Data accessed through YouTube: the channel name and ID, and the ID, title, description and statistics of the owner's own videos. No data from other channels or users is accessed.
  • Storage and deletion: this data is stored only in local files on the owner's computer, is never shared, and is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Public statistics may be kept longer only as allowed by the YouTube API Services additional policies for derived metrics and data storage.
  • Cookies and device data: BF6 Clips does not use cookies and does not collect information from any device other than the owner's computer.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. After revoking, the stored token stops working and the local data can be deleted.
